Key benefits

  • The zones are on the chart. Nobody has to imagine where the quadrants are.
  • They follow the data. Lines at the average or median move with every filter.
  • A click answers "who". A bubble or a zone filters every other visual on the page.
  • It stays inside Power BI. No telemetry, no endpoints, no CDN.

Tips

  • Use a measure for ratios: DIVIDE(SUM(Margin), SUM(Sales)). A % column gets summed per bubble.
  • Average and median count each bubble once: for a weighted reference, type a fixed value.
  • Log scale needs every value on that axis above 0; a line at 0 sits on the edge.
  • Coming from Asymmetric Matrix 1.0? Lower half → One zone and Colour by → Each category.
