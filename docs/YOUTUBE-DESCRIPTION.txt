TITLE
Bubble & Scatter Chart for Power BI — Quadrants, Asymmetric Zones and a Zone Table

DESCRIPTION
400 customers on a scatter chart, and the question is still open: which ones matter? A plain scatter draws the dots. It does not split them into segments, it does not tell you which channel lands where, and it treats your business as if it were symmetric.

Bubble & Scatter Chart by TCViz turns the scatter into a segmentation: quadrants centred on the average or the median, zones you name and colour, and — in Pro — different dividers above and below the line, plus a table that crosses your legend with the zones.
