Asymmetric Matrix for Power BI — Full walkthrough (Free & Pro)

A standard 2×2 matrix forces equal zones on both sides. The Asymmetric Matrix breaks that constraint: define a different number of vertical zones above and below the horizontal reference line — three above, two below, four above, one below — whatever your analysis actually needs.

In this video I walk through the complete setup, from adding your first fields to configuring asymmetric lower zones, axis titles, fixed axes, data labels, and zone count cards.

FREE vs PRO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Free includes:
✅ Scatter plot with up to 5 upper zones
✅ Horizontal Y-reference line (value, color, style)
✅ Category color grouping
✅ Bubble size field
✅ Tooltips (up to 10 fields)
✅ Cross-filter on zone click

Pro adds:
⭐ Lower zone asymmetry (up to 5 independent zones below)
⭐ Axis titles (X & Y)
⭐ Fixed axis range (stable during cross-filtering)
⭐ Data labels on points
⭐ Zone count cards
