License

The Pro features are unlocked via Microsoft AppSource license management (plan "pro" of this offer). A Pro license is required per user. Licenses are managed through your Microsoft 365 / Power BI admin portal.

The free features never depend on a licence and are never blocked. While a report is being edited without a licence, Pro features that are turned on may be shown as a preview under a "Pro preview" watermark. In reading view, published reports and environments where licences cannot be checked, they are not applied without an active licence.
