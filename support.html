Bubble & Scatter Chart plots two measures against each other — one bubble per customer, product or project — and divides the plot into zones you define: a horizontal Y line and up to four vertical dividers. The Y line and divider 1 can sit at a fixed value, the average or the median; the other dividers take fixed values. Bubbles can be sized by a third measure and coloured by group. Click a bubble, a zone or a group to filter the rest of the report.

The native Power BI scatter chart draws the points. This visual adds the segmentation you read them with: named, coloured zones; quadrants that follow the average as filters change; and, in Pro, a different set of dividers below the line than above it, plus a table that counts each group in each zone.

Quick start

  1. Add Bubble & Scatter Chart from AppSource (Visualizations pane → Get more visuals).
  2. Drag the item you want one bubble for — customer, product, project — into Category.
  3. Drag two measures into X axis and Y axis. The chart appears.
  4. Optional: a measure in Size (bubble area), a column in Legend (colour by group), measures in Tooltips.
  5. In the tips bar at the bottom, click Quadrants at the average, or pick a style template.

With no fields, the visual shows a start screen with these steps, the Pro features and links to this guide, the product page and the video; the links open only when you click them. While you edit, a tips bar at the bottom offers the templates, “Quadrants at the average” and the “Try Pro” buttons. Hide it with ✕; it comes back from Format → Style template → Show setup tips. It never shows in reading view or in very small tiles.

Lines and zones

The Y line splits the plot into an upper and a lower half. The upper dividers (0 to 4 vertical lines) split the upper half into up to five zones. The lower half has three layouts:

Lower halfWhat it drawsTier
Same dividers as aboveThe upper dividers continue below the line: with one divider, four quadrants. The default.Free
One zoneThe whole lower half is one zone, as in Asymmetric Matrix 1.0.Free
Own dividersA different number of dividers, at different positions, below the line — the asymmetric zones. Up to five zones per half.Pro

The Y line can sit at a fixed value, the average or the median of the bubbles' Y values. Divider 1 of the upper half can do the same with X; dividers 2 to 4, and the lower dividers, take fixed values. Average and median are recalculated from the bubbles currently in the visual, so they move when the report is filtered.

Zones are named Zone A, Zone B… from left to right across the upper half, and continue in the lower half (in quadrants: A and B above, C and D below). Give each one your own label and colour in the Upper zones and Lower zones cards. A divider outside the data range widens the axis so its zone stays visible.

Pro preview

Without a licence you can try every Pro feature on your own data:

  • While you edit, a Pro setting you turn on (in the Format pane or with a “Try Pro” button in the tips bar) is drawn working, under a “Pro preview” watermark that names it, and Power BI shows its own licence notice with the way to get a licence.
  • In reading view, readers without a licence see the free result: no watermark, and none of the Pro features (own lower dividers fall back to the quadrants).
  • Where licences cannot be read (Publish to Web, embedding, export to PDF or PowerPoint), the free result is drawn, with no watermark and no notice.

The setting you turned on is kept. Once the licence is active, the feature works everywhere and the watermark goes.

On-object formatting

With Format on, click the bubbles, data labels, axis values, axis titles, plot area, Y line, a zone, the zone labels, the legend, the trend line, the zone counts or the zone table to see their main options next to them, with a link to the card in the Format pane. In format mode a click chooses what to format and does not filter.

Use cases

1. Customer portfolio: value and margin by channel

Category = Customer, X = Sales, Y = Margin % (a DIVIDE measure), Size = Orders, Legend = Channel. Click Quadrants at the average: high-value, high-margin customers sit top right; large accounts with thin margins bottom right. With Pro, the zone table set to % of row shows what share of each channel sits in each quadrant, and the legend share tells you the channel mix of the zone you click.

2. Project prioritisation

Category = Project, X = Effort, Y = Expected value, Size = Budget. Y line at the value threshold the committee uses. Above it, two dividers split effort into Quick wins, Plan and Major bets; below it, with Pro own dividers, a single divider separates Fill-ins from Avoid. Click a zone to list its projects in a table beside the chart.

3. Risk register

Category = Risk, X = Likelihood, Y = Impact, Legend = Owner or Area. High-impact risks need finer bands of likelihood than low-impact ones: three zones above the impact line, two below (Pro). Zone counts show how many risks fall in each band, and fixed axes keep the scale steady while you filter by area.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? What does Pro add?

The chart is complete without a licence: bubbles, size, colours, data labels, axis titles, Y line, up to four dividers, quadrants, log scale, legend, templates and all interaction. Pro adds own lower dividers (asymmetric zones), the zone table, the trend line, zone counts and fixed axis range. See Free and Pro.

How do I buy Pro?

From the offer on Microsoft AppSource, plan pro, or from the notice Power BI shows when you use a Pro feature while editing. Prices are shown on the AppSource listing.

I bought Pro and still see the watermark

The licence is assigned in the Microsoft 365 admin center to the user who opens the report. Once assigned, close and reopen Power BI Desktop or reload the report in the service so Power BI reads the licence again. A new licence can take a while to be recognised. If it persists, write to support@tcviz.com.

Do readers of my report need a Pro licence?

The licence is checked for the person viewing the report. Viewers without one see the free result with no watermark: the report still works for them, without the Pro features.

What is “Pro preview”, and why do I see it on my chart?

You turned on a Pro setting without a licence. While you edit it is drawn working under a watermark so you can judge it on your own data; readers see the free result. Turn the setting off, or get the Pro plan to remove the watermark. See Pro preview.

Why do I see only one bubble?

There is nothing in Category (or Legend), so Power BI aggregates the whole table into one point. Put the field you want one bubble for — customer, product, project — in Category.

My percentages are huge or wrong. Why?

Power BI aggregates X, Y and Size for each bubble. If you drag a percentage column, it is summed per bubble: a customer with ten rows at 30% shows 300%. Averaging is not right either, because it ignores the size of each row. Create a measure that divides the totals, e.g. Margin % = DIVIDE(SUM(Sales[Margin]), SUM(Sales[Sales])), and use it instead. The same applies to prices, rates and any ratio.

I have more bubbles than customers

With both Category and Legend, there is one bubble per combination. If a customer appears under two channels, it gets two bubbles. Use a Legend field that has one value per Category value (e.g. the customer's main channel).

How is the bubble size calculated?

The area of each bubble is proportional to its Size value, relative to the largest value in view: a bubble with half the value has half the area, not half the width. Blank, zero and negative values get the smallest size. Bubbles → Size scales them all, and in small tiles they shrink so the largest stays readable.

How do I get a classic four-quadrant chart?

Click Quadrants at the average in the tips bar, or pick the Four quadrants template. By hand: Y line at Average, Upper zones → Number of dividers = 1, Divider 1 at Average, Lower half = Same dividers as above.

What is the difference between quadrants and own dividers?

With Same dividers as above (free), the upper dividers continue below the Y line, so both halves are split at the same X values. With Own dividers (Pro), the lower half has its own number and position of dividers: for example three bands of sales above the margin line and two below. Use it when the segmentation that matters is different for each half.

How many zones can I have?

Up to five per half: four dividers above the line, and four below (as quadrants, or with Pro own dividers).

Is the average weighted?

No. Average and median are taken over the bubbles, one value per bubble, without weighting by Size. A small customer counts as much as a large one. If you need a weighted reference (for example the company's overall margin), type it as a Fixed value.

Do the average and median lines move when I filter?

Yes. They are recalculated from the bubbles in the visual after report, page and visual filters. Selecting bubbles or a cross-highlight from another visual does not move them.

Can dividers 2 to 4 follow the average?

No. Divider 1 of the upper half can be at a fixed value, the average or the median; the other dividers, and the lower dividers, take fixed values.

I turned on log scale and the axis is still linear

A logarithmic axis needs every value above 0. If any bubble on that axis is zero or negative, the axis is drawn linear and, while you edit, a note says “Log scale needs values above 0: shown linear”. The Y line does not block log Y: a Y line at 0 or below (including the default fixed value 0) sits on the bottom edge of the plot. With Fixed axis range on, values seen earlier also count.

Can a divider be at 0 or below with a log X axis?

Yes. A divider at 0 or below does not block log X: with every bubble above 0, that zone is simply empty and the divider is not drawn.

How does Fixed axis range work? Pro

The axes keep the widest range seen since the page was opened: they grow when a filter brings larger values and never shrink. Bubbles then move against the same scale as you change slicers, so the positions compare. The range resets when you change the fields in X axis or Y axis, or when the page is opened again.

What do the zone table modes mean? Pro

% of row: how each group is spread across the zones (each row adds to 100%). % of column: the mix of groups inside each zone (each column adds to 100%). Points: the number of bubbles. Size field: the sum of the Size measure. See The zone table in detail.

Why can't I build the zone table with a matrix visual?

Because the zones are not in your data model. They are drawn by this visual, often at the average or median of what is on screen, and they move with every filter. Only the visual knows which bubble is in which zone at each moment.

The zone table does not appear

Check Zone table → Show zone table and make the visual larger: the table is hidden in very small tiles. Without a Legend field it shows a single row, All. Without a licence it appears only while editing, under the watermark.

What does the trend line calculate? Pro

A least-squares straight line of Y on X over the bubbles in view, unweighted, with its R² in the top-right corner. On logarithmic axes the same line is drawn as a curve. It needs at least two bubbles, and R² at least three.

What do the zone counts show? Pro

The number of bubbles in each zone, in a badge at its top-left corner, and optionally the % of all bubbles in the visual.

What happens when I click a bubble, a zone or the legend?

A bubble filters the report by that point; a zone background selects all of its bubbles; a legend item selects its group. Ctrl adds to the selection; clicking an empty area clears it. See Interaction.

Do bookmarks keep the selection?

Yes. A bookmark restores the selected bubbles, and other visuals stay filtered by them.

Can I use it with the keyboard?

Yes: Tab into the chart, arrow keys between zones, Enter or Space to select a zone, Escape to clear, Shift+F10 for the context menu.

I added a Legend field but there is no legend

The legend shows when Bubbles → Colour by is Auto (legend if any) and Legend → Show legend is on. With One colour, Each category or Zone, the bubbles do not follow the groups, so there is no legend to show.

Can I colour bubbles with conditional formatting (fx)?

Yes: Bubbles → Colour → fx, with a rule or a field. A rule takes precedence over Colour by.

What does a style template change, and does it keep my settings?

A template sets colours and styles only where you have not set them yourself. When you choose a template, the settings it defines are reset so it looks as designed — including a fixed bubble colour or an fx rule on bubble colour; add the rule again afterwards if you need it. Your lines (Y line, dividers, lower half) are never reset. Four quadrants places the Y line and divider 1 at the average only if you have not set them.

How do I hide the tips bar?

Click ✕ on the bar, or turn off Format → Style template → Show setup tips. It only shows while you edit.

How are numbers formatted?

With each measure's format string, in the report's language, on the axes, tooltips and zone table. Axes → Decimals at 0–5 overrides the decimals on the axes; −1 keeps the model format. On a log axis each tick carries its own unit (1K, 10K, 1M).

How do I change or hide the axis titles?

They show the field names by default. Type your own in Axes → X title / Y title, or turn off Axis titles.

Some data labels are missing

By design: a label that would overlap another label or fall outside the plot is not drawn. Hover the bubble for the tooltip, lower the label size, or filter to fewer bubbles.

What changed from Asymmetric Matrix 1.0?

The visual was renamed and rewritten. It has the same ID, and every 1.0 setting kept its name, so saved reports open with their settings. Visible differences in a 1.0 report:

  • The lower half defaults to quadrants (same dividers as above). For the 1.0 look, choose Lower zones → Lower half → One zone.
  • Bubbles default to one colour. For the 1.0 look (one colour per category), choose Bubbles → Colour by → Each category.
  • Axis titles appear with the field names. Turn them off in Axes → Axis titles.
  • Clicking a bubble now filters by that point; clicking a zone background still selects the zone.
  • Data labels and axis titles are free now.
My 1.0 report had dividers below the line. What happens now?

They are read as Own dividers, a Pro feature, exactly as in 1.0. With a licence they are drawn as before. Without one, readers see the quadrants (the upper dividers continued below), and while editing you see your lower dividers under the Pro preview watermark.

How many rows can it plot?

Up to 30,000 rows (bubbles). Beyond that Power BI reduces the data.

Does it support report page tooltips?

Yes. Set a tooltip page in Format → General → Tooltips. The default tooltip shows Category, Legend, X, Y, Size and up to 10 Tooltips measures.

What does “No data for the current filters” mean?

The filters leave no bubble with both an X and a Y value. Clear a filter or check that the measures return values for the current selection.

Does it work in high contrast mode?

Yes. Bubbles, lines, zones and texts take the colours of the Windows high-contrast theme, and the selection is marked with the theme's selection colour.

Does the visual send my data anywhere?

No. It makes no network calls, has no telemetry, sets no cookies and stores nothing outside the report. The links on the start screen open only when you click them, through Power BI. See the privacy policy.
